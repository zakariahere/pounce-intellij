Reports a Future that is already running when IO.fromFuture receives it: IO.fromFuture(IO.pure(future)) or a Future created in a val outside the effect. The effect can then neither delay, retry nor cancel it.

Before:

IO.fromFuture(IO.pure(client.call()))

After (quick fix Suspend the Future construction (IO(...) instead of IO.pure)):

IO.fromFuture(IO(client.call()))

A val f = client.call() used once inside IO(f) gets Move the Future construction inside the effect. IO(future) that returns a Future nobody awaits is reported with Wrap in IO.fromFuture.
