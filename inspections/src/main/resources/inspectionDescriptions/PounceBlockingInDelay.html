Reports a blocking call (JDBC, java.io, java.nio.file.Files, Thread.sleep, Await.result, scala.io.Source, sockets, Future#get, ...) inside IO(...), IO.delay or Sync[F].delay. Delay runs on the compute pool, which has one thread per core: blocking it starves every other fiber.

Before:

IO(Files.readString(path))

After (quick fix Replace with IO.blocking):

IO.blocking(Files.readString(path))

Interruptible calls (sleeps, waits, socket reads) also get Replace with IO.interruptible, and Thread.sleep(n) gets Replace with IO.sleep. The list of blocking APIs is data (BlockingCalls.scala); in-memory streams such as ByteArrayInputStream are excluded. Calls already inside blocking / interruptible are not reported.
